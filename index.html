ABOUT

Legend in Blue

Virat Kohli is an Indian cricketer and former captain of the Indian national team. Known for his aggressive style, disciplined fitness, and relentless ambition, he transformed into one of the most feared batsmen in world cricket.

From thrilling chases to record-breaking centuries, Kohli’s journey reflects consistency, leadership, and a passion for the game that inspires millions.

  • Born 5 November 1988
  • Role Top-order batter
  • Format Test, ODI, T20
  • Teams India, RCB
