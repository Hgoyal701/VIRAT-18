About

Virat Kohli is an Indian international cricketer and former captain of the India national team. Known for his aggressive batting, keen fitness and consistency across formats, Kohli has set and broken numerous records in international cricket.

  • Born: 5 November 1988
  • Role: Top-order batsman
  • Batting: Right-hand bat
  • International Debut: 2008 (ODI)
